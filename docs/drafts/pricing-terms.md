## en: Packages, free trial and payment

1. **Packages.** moarge offers Solo (19 ₾ a month, 1 specialist), Business (59 ₾ a month,
   10 specialists, each extra 9 ₾ a month, up to 50) and Multi-branch (149 ₾ a month, 3 locations,
   each extra 40 ₾ a month; available later). Current prices are at moarge.com/pricing.
   **[check] whether prices include VAT.**
2. **When prices apply.** Paid packages apply from 1 December 2026. Salons that sign up in
   November 2026 use moarge free for 30 days.
3. **Free trial.** Salons that sign up from 1 December 2026 get a 7-day free trial, with no card
   and up to 10 specialists.
4. **After the trial.** If no package is chosen and paid by the end of the trial, the account
   becomes view only. Data is not deleted. **[decide] how long data is kept on an unpaid account
   (the code currently says 30 days).**
5. **Billing periods.** Pay monthly, for 6 months (10% off) or for 12 months (20% off), in
   advance. **[decide] whether monthly payment renews automatically or is paid by hand each time.**
6. **December discount.** In December 2026, the first 20 salons to buy a package get 20% off
   their first payment, on top of the 6 or 12 month discount. Order is set by the time the payment
   is confirmed.
7. **SMS.** Business includes 1,000 SMS a month, Multi-branch 3,000. Included SMS renew every
   billing month and unused ones do not carry over. Extra SMS can be bought on any package, at
   least 1,000 at a time, 20 ₾ per 1,000. Bought SMS never expire and carry over. Included SMS are
   used first, then bought ones. When SMS run out, SMS reminders stop until more are bought; there
   are no automatic extra charges. **[check] a long or Georgian-script message may count as more
   than one SMS (uBill's rules).**
8. **How to pay.** By card through Flitt. moarge does not store card details.
9. **Cancelling.** You can cancel any time. The account stays active until the end of the paid
   period. **[decide] refunds: none / unused full months refunded / refund within X days.**
10. **Price changes.** We tell you by email at least **[decide] 30** days before a price change.
    The price of a period you have already paid does not change.
